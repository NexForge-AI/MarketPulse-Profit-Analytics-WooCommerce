### 🧮 5 Accounting Formulas Demystified

The plugin follows standard GAAP and IFRS e-commerce accounting standards:

1. **Gross Revenue** = `Retail Price × Quantity Sold`
2. **Total COGS** = `Wholesale Cost × Quantity Sold`
3. **Gross Profit** = `Gross Revenue − Total COGS`
4. **Courier Deficit** = `Actual Courier Cost − Shipping Fee Paid by Customer`
5. **True Net Profit** = `Gross Profit − Payment Gateway Fee − Courier Deficit − Allocated OpEx`

**Net Margin %** = `(True Net Profit ÷ Gross Revenue) × 100%`

== Installation ==

### Automatic Installation (Recommended)
1. Log in to your WordPress admin dashboard.
2. Navigate to **Plugins > Add New**.
3. Search for **"Simple Cost of Goods"** or **"Profit Tracker for WooCommerce"**.
4. Click **Install Now**, then click **Activate**.
5. Go to **WooCommerce > Profit Analytics** to view your real-time executive dashboard.

### Manual Installation
1. Download the `simple-cost-of-goods-for-woocommerce.zip` file.
2. In your WordPress admin, go to **Plugins > Add New > Upload Plugin**.
3. Choose the downloaded ZIP file and click **Install Now**.
4. Activate the plugin.

### Getting Started in 3 Steps
1. **Set Cost Prices:** Open any product under **Products > All Products** and enter your wholesale purchase cost in the **Cost Price (COGS)** field.
2. **Review Gateway Fees:** Go to **WooCommerce > Profit Analytics > Payment Gateways & Costs** to verify your processing rates (pre-configured defaults for Stripe, PayPal, bKash, Nagad, COD).
3. **Track Real Profit:** Watch your True Net Profit update automatically as new customer orders arrive!

== Frequently Asked Questions ==

= Does this plugin slow down my store or affect customer checkout speed? =
Absolutely not! The plugin is engineered with a strict zero-frontend footprint policy. Exactly 0 JavaScript files and 0 CSS stylesheets are loaded on your store's frontend, catalog, cart, or checkout pages. All calculations run strictly in the WordPress admin and during backend order transitions.

= If I change a product cost price today, will it alter past order profits? =
Never. When an order is placed, unit wholesale costs, gateway fees, and shipping metrics are permanently locked into that specific order's immutable financial snapshot. Updating a product's cost price today will only apply to future orders, safeguarding your historical accounting records.

= Is this plugin compatible with WooCommerce High-Performance Order Storage (HPOS)? =
Yes! Simple Cost of Goods & Profit Tracker declares 100% native compatibility with High-Performance Order Storage (`custom_order_tables`). It works flawlessly whether your store runs in modern HPOS mode or legacy post-meta mode.

= Do I need to manually configure payment gateway fees? =
No. The plugin includes pre-configured standard transaction fees for major global and regional payment processors, including Stripe (2.9% + 30¢), PayPal (3.49% + 49¢), Square (2.6% + 10¢), bKash (1.5%), Nagad (1.2%), Rocket (1.5%), SSLCommerz (2.5%), and Cash on Delivery (COD). If you have negotiated custom volume rates with your processor, you can easily adjust them in the settings tab.

= How does Courier Shipping Deficit tracking work? =
If you charge your customer a flat $5.00 for delivery, but FedEx, DHL, Pathao, or Steadfast invoices you $8.50, you have absorbed a $3.50 shipping deficit. The plugin calculates this shortfall on each order and deducts it directly from your Net Profit.

= Can I track non-product overhead expenses like Facebook Ads and store hosting? =
Yes! The built-in **Operating Expenses (OpEx)** tab lets you record one-time and recurring monthly business overheads (ad spend, staff salaries, software subscriptions, rent) to calculate true company take-home profit.

= Can I bulk update cost prices with CSV spreadsheets? =
Yes. The plugin integrates directly into WooCommerce's native Product CSV Importer and Exporter, adding `Cost of Goods (COGS)` and `Supplier` columns so you can manage thousands of SKUs in Microsoft Excel or Google Sheets.

= How do I export financial reports for my accountant or tax filing? =
In **WooCommerce > Profit Analytics > Overview**, select your desired date range (e.g., This Month, Last Month, or Custom), then click **Export P&L (CSV)**. The resulting spreadsheet is formatted for accountants and tax auditors.

= What is the difference between Gross Profit and True Net Profit? =
Gross Profit only subtracts the wholesale product cost (COGS) from your selling price. True Net Profit goes much deeper by deducting Payment Gateway Transaction Fees, Courier Shipping Deficits, and allocated Operating Expenses (OpEx) — revealing the exact cash that actually stays in your bank account.

Yes, 100% free and open-source under the GNU General Public License v2 (GPL-2.0).
